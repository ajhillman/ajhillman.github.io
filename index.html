Andrew Jonathan Hillman, Dallas healthcare compliance expert witness and senior paralegal

Andrew Jonathan Hillman is a senior paralegal, not an attorney, and does not give legal advice. Expert engagements are arranged only through retaining counsel.

Andrew Jonathan Hillman is a Dallas healthcare compliance and regulatory expert witness, retained through counsel, and a senior paralegal. He gives operator testimony on healthcare operations, compliance and federal regulatory matters, and prepares expert reports intended to meet the standards of Federal Rule of Evidence 702.

His opinions draw on three decades of building and operating FDA-regulated healthcare businesses, including surgical hospitals, ambulatory surgical centers, clinical laboratories, pharmacy operations and medical-device businesses. Subject areas include the Stark Law, the Anti-Kickback Statute, HIPAA, the 60-day Medicare and Medicaid overpayment rule, False Claims Act risk in healthcare, and FDA and 510(k) medical device frameworks.

Engagements as a consulting or testifying expert are accepted only through retaining counsel, and conflicts are reviewed before any engagement begins.
